# Delete your Valore account

You can delete your account and **all linked data** at any time, directly from the app.

## From the app

1. Open **Valore**.
2. Tap the **profile** icon at the top right of the Home screen.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm.

Deletion is immediate and **cannot be undone**.

## What gets deleted

- your profile (name, city, market, platforms);
- all valued items, with their **photos**, prices, history and alerts;
- generated listings;
- the usage log (scans and credits used).

Database backups are overwritten within 30 days.

## Subscription and credits

**Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription**: subscriptions are managed by Apple. Before deleting your account, cancel from **Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Valore**.
Unused credits are lost when the account is deleted.

## No longer have the app?

If you have uninstalled the app and want us to delete your data, write to us at [info@toot.srl](mailto:info@toot.srl) or by PEC at [toot@kelipec.it](mailto:toot@kelipec.it). We will ask you for some information to identify your account (for example the approximate date you used the app and the items you valued), because the account is anonymous.
